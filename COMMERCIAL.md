## Do I need one?

**You don't need a commercial licence** to:

- use the `stratazip` command, at home or at work, to make, open, verify and repair
  archives, including on company servers and in internal scripts;
- study, modify and share StrataZip under the AGPL;
- build it into a project that is itself released under the AGPL-3.0 (or the GPL-3.0,
  as the AGPL allows).

**You need a commercial licence** if you:

- ship StrataZip, or any part of its code, inside a product that is not open source
  (an application, an appliance, firmware, a library or SDK);
- offer a network service built on a modified StrataZip and do not want to publish
  your modifications, as the AGPL's section 13 would require;
- work somewhere whose policy does not allow AGPL software, and want the same program
  under ordinary commercial terms.

If you are unsure, open a [discussion](../../discussions) and describe what you are
building: we will tell you honestly whether you need one.

## How it works

The model is the one JetBrains made familiar:

- **Perpetual.** The version you buy is yours to use forever, under the commercial
  terms, even if you never pay again.
- **12 months of updates included.** Every release made in the year after your purchase
  is covered by the same licence.
- **Renew if you want newer versions.** A renewal adds another 12 months of updates. If
  you let it lapse, keep using what you have; you can renew later.
- **No licence keys in the program.** StrataZip never phones home and never checks a key.
  The licence is a legal grant, recorded on your receipt; the program is identical for
  everyone.
